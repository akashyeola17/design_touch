ABOUT DESIGN TOUCH

Creating Spaces. Building Brand Experiences.

Design Touch is an exhibition stall design and turnkey execution company based in Ghatkopar East, Mumbai. We create impactful brand spaces for exhibitions, trade shows, and corporate events, from the first layout through fabrication and on-site installation.

With 14+ years of industry experience, we bring together creative design, technical expertise, and seamless execution. Exhibition environments are planned to be visually engaging, functional, and aligned with each brand's identity—whether the brief is a custom exhibition stall, an expo booth, or a complete event setup.

From concept and 3D visualization to stall fabrication, installation, and on-site execution, the team manages every stage with a focus on quality, timelines, and detail. The same process supports interior design, event environments, and branding displays when a project needs more than the stall itself.

See how that work looks in our exhibition stall design projects, or start a conversation for an upcoming show anywhere in India.
