ABOUT DESIGN TOUCH

Creating Spaces. Building Brand Experiences.

Design Touch is an exhibition stall design and turnkey execution company specializing in creating impactful brand spaces for exhibitions, trade shows and corporate events.

With 14+ years of industry experience, we bring together creative design, technical expertise and seamless execution to deliver exhibition environments that are visually engaging, functional and aligned with each brand's identity.

From concept and 3D visualization to fabrication, installation and on-site execution, our team manages every stage of the process with a strong focus on quality, timelines and attention to detail.
