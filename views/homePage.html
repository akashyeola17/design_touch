Project FAQs

Questions,
answered.

Planning a trade show or expo? These answers cover how Design Touch designs, fabricates, and installs exhibition stalls from Mumbai for events across India.

01What exhibition stall services does Design Touch provide?+

We handle exhibition stall design, custom stall fabrication, 3D visualization, graphics, installation, and dismantling. The same team can also support interior design, event environments, and branding displays when a brief needs more than the booth itself.

02Do you both design and fabricate exhibition stalls?+

Yes. Design Touch is an exhibition stall contractor as well as a design studio. Concept, 3D design, fabrication, and on-site execution stay in one workflow so the built stall matches the approved design.

03Is Design Touch only available for exhibitions in Mumbai?+

Our studio is in Ghatkopar East, Mumbai. We design and fabricate from this base and execute exhibition stalls, expo booths, and event setups for clients across India, including Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, and Pune.

04How does the stall design and fabrication process work?+

We start with your brand, space size, and event goals, then develop a layout and 3D exhibition design. After approval we fabricate, brand, install, and support the stall on site. To begin, share your event dates and stall size through the quote button or WhatsApp.

05Can you design custom exhibition booths for trade shows and expos?+

Yes. We build custom exhibition stalls and expo booths around your products, visitor flow, and branding rather than repeating a standard shell. See our project portfolio for recent exhibition work.
